# Chinese Zodiac Sign

## Activity Description

This activity is a Python program that determines a person's Chinese Zodiac sign based on their year of birth.

The program uses 1900 as the baseline year, which is the Year of the Rat. The zodiac signs repeat every 12 years.

## Requirements

- Ask the user to enter their year of birth.
- The baseline year is 1900.
- Validate that the year is not earlier than 1900.
- If the year is earlier than 1900, display an appropriate invalid-year message and stop the program.
- Determine the Chinese Zodiac sign based on the year of birth.
- Use the following 12-year cycle:

1. Rat (鼠 / Shu)
2. Ox (牛 / Niu)
3. Tiger (虎 / Hu)
4. Rabbit (兔 / Tu)
5. Dragon (龙 / Long)
6. Snake (蛇 / She)
7. Horse (马 / Ma)
8. Goat (羊 / Yang)
9. Monkey (猴 / Hou)
10. Rooster (鸡 / Ji)
11. Dog (狗 / Gou)
12. Pig (猪 / Zhu)

- Consider only the year of birth.
